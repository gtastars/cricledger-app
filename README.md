# CricLedger

The cricket scoring app for match day. Score every ball, see live scorecards and player stats, and keep a history of your games. No sign-up, and your matches stay on your phone.

## Get the app (Android)

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/usmanAfzalKhan/cricledger-app/releases/latest)**

1. Open the link on your Android phone and tap the **CricLedger .apk** file.
2. When the download finishes, tap it to open.
3. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap **Install**.

Already have CricLedger? Installing the new version updates it and keeps your saved matches.
